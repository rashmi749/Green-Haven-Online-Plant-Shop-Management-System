# Green-Haven-Online-Plant-Shop-Management-System
online plant shop management system that simplifies plant sales, order processing, inventory management, and customer services.
📖 Introduction
Green Haven is a web-based e‑commerce platform designed for selling plants and plant accessories online. It provides customers with a modern, user-friendly interface to browse, customize, and order plants, while giving administrators powerful tools to manage products, orders, and business insights.

✨ Features
🛒 Customer Features
User registration & login

Browse plants by categories (Indoor, Outdoor, Succulents, Accessories)

Product customization (pot type, soil mix, size, add-ons)

Shopping cart & secure checkout

Wishlist & favorites

Advanced search & filters (price, plant type, care level)

Reviews & ratings for products

Delivery tracking with real-time updates

Plant care guides & tips section

Subscription service (e.g., “Plant of the Month”)

Gift options with personalized notes

Multi-language support (Sinhala, Tamil, English)

Social sharing (Instagram/Facebook integration)

Plant matching quiz (suggests plants based on lifestyle)

Care reminders (watering, fertilizing, repotting alerts)

AR/VR plant preview (visualize plants in customer’s space)

📊 Admin Features
Admin dashboard with secure login

Product & category management

Order management (view/update status, delivery tracking)

Inventory management (stock alerts, low-stock notifications)

Weekly & automated reports (sales, orders, customer insights)

Analytics dashboard (top-selling plants, customer demographics)

Promotions & discounts management

Customer management (profiles, order history)

Multi-admin roles (manager, staff)

Customer support panel (FAQs, inquiries, complaints)

Supplier management (track suppliers, purchase orders, restocking)

AI-powered recommendations (suggest trending plants/bundles)

Dynamic pricing (adjust prices based on demand/season)

SEO & marketing tools (blog optimization, email campaigns, Google Analytics integration)

🔐 Security & Performance
Encrypted login & password storage

SSL-secured transactions

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for admin accounts

Role-based access control

Optimized database queries for fast loading

Backup & restore system

Cloud hosting (AWS/Azure) for scalability

Data encryption for customer & payment info

⚙️ Technology Stack
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript 

Backend: PHP 

Database: MySQL

Tools: XAMPP, Visual Studio Code

📄 Pages & Sections
Homepage – Hero banner (“Bring Nature Home”), featured categories, seasonal offers

Shop Page – Filter by category, search bar, product grid

Product Page – Plant details, customization options, add to cart

Cart & Checkout – Cart summary, payment gateway, delivery info

Admin Panel – Dashboard, product management, order management, reports

Tips & Guides – Plant care articles, seasonal inspiration

Contact Page – Contact form, shop location, social media links

🎯 Expected Outcomes
Faster, more accurate plant order handling

Reduced manual errors

Improved customer experience with online ordering

Business insights via weekly & automated reports

Scalable platform for future growth

Strong customer engagement through personalization & community features
